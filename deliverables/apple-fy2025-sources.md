# Apple FY2025 Environmental Progress — source notes

The presentation uses Apple’s 2026 Environmental Progress Report, which reports calendar/fiscal 2025 environmental results, together with Apple’s April 16, 2026 Newsroom release.

## Primary sources

- [Apple 2026 Environmental Progress Report](https://www.apple.com/environment/pdf/Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2026.pdf)
- [Apple accelerates progress with highest-ever recycled material in its products](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/apple-accelerates-progress-with-highest-ever-recycled-material-in-its-products/)
- [Apple’s $4.7B in Green Bonds support innovative green technology](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/03/apples-four-point-seven-billion-in-green-bonds-support-innovative-green-technology/)

## Claim precision applied in the deck

- The `30%` materials metric is described as **recycled content across products shipped in 2025**, matching Apple’s reported definition.
- The value-chain metric is described as **more than 60% lower gross emissions than 2015**, toward a **75% reduction target by 2030**. It is not characterized as “60% carbon neutral.”
- The packaging milestone is described as **100% fiber-based packaging**. Apple excludes inks, coatings, and adhesives; residual prior packaging inventory represented less than 0.4% of the FY2025 packaging footprint.
- The zero-waste statement is qualified to **established final-assembly supplier sites**, matching Apple’s third-party verification boundary.
- The gross-footprint mix uses Apple’s reported categories: `53%` product manufacturing, `27%` product use, `16%` product transport, `3%` business operations, and `<1%` end-of-life. Scope 1 and Scope 2 are each also `<1%`; totals may exceed 100% because of rounding.
- The `43M metric tons` figure is identified as **estimated avoided emissions from FY2025 environmental programs**, not as the year-over-year reduction in Apple’s footprint.
- The green-bond narrative does not claim a lower cost of capital; Apple’s public reporting supports cumulative issuance and project funding, but not that causal outcome.
